    How it works

    1. Clean

    Lowercase, strip links, source tags, symbols.

    2. Vectorise

    TF-IDF with word pairs (n-grams) turns text into numbers.

    3. Compare

    Four models are trained and ranked by cross-validation.

    4. Explain

    Top words and style signals are shown with each verdict.
